# Heroes of Sardath

A fantasy adventure for the browser, for one or two players. George the Wizard and Rory the Moonfox take turns on the same screen (or play solo and the computer runs the other hero): dice duels in the style of the Fighting Fantasy books, treasure, tricky choices and a siege battle in each chapter.

**Play it:** https://omniwolf.github.io/heroes-of-sardath/

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. You can also download it and double-click it to play offline.
